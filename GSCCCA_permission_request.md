We're happy to discuss rate limits, IP whitelisting, a signed addendum to

## Notes for Sharon, not part of the letter

- **Lead with the account you already have.** A named Premium subscriber
  asking for a narrow exception reads as a real business, not a scraper
  looking for cover. That's worth more than any argument about volume.
- **Name a specific rate, and give them an easy fallback.** A vague "let
  us automate GSCCCA" is easy to decline. "Book-and-page, hourly,
  statewide -- or these ten counties if statewide's too much" gives them
  a yes they can actually say, at whichever scope they're comfortable
  granting, instead of one all-or-nothing ask.
- **The ten counties are Georgia's most populous** (Fulton, Gwinnett,
  Cobb, DeKalb, Chatham, Clayton, Cherokee, Forsyth, Henry, Hall --
  confirmed against 2024 Census data 2026-09-10), not just the four this
  pipeline currently touches. Cherokee and Cobb are already in scope;
  Fulton is too. The other seven would be new counties to build sources
  for if this comes through -- worth remembering before assuming the win
  is "free."
- **If they counter with a licensing fee or a different mechanism, take
  it.** That's the outcome this letter is trying to produce. A paid,
  sanctioned feed beats free access you could lose without notice.
- **If they say no, or don't respond in a month, that's your answer.**
  Don't quietly proceed anyway. The pipeline already works without
  GSCCCA — this letter is upside, not a dependency.
